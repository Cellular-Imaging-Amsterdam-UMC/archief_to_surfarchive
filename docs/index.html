↓ Download for Windows Quick start →

Windows x64 · Standalone .exe · SURF Data Archive account required

Your first archive

Have your SURF Data Archive account and a folder you want to keep.

  1. 01

    Open and sign in

    Download and open the Windows app. Sign in with your SURF Data Archive username and password.

  2. 02

    Choose source and destination

    Select a folder on the left in Local / NAS. Open the destination on the right in SURF Data Archive.

  3. 03

    Queue the archive

    Choose Archive to SURF… and confirm the options. When the job starts, Scan source appears in Activity and the app checks how much staging space it needs.

Want to keep your local files?

Clear Remove originals in the archive confirmation. After the SURF checks pass, a folder archive leaves a local __sda_ reference folder with the contents and retention files.

Get your files back

Open the archive folder in the SURF pane. __sda_contents.html shows what was packed.

  1. Restore if needed. For items on tape, choose Restore from tape and wait for Ready to download in Activity.
  2. Download the parts. Put all numbered ZIP parts in the same local folder.
  3. Extract with 7-Zip. Open the file ending in .zip.001 and choose Extract. 7-Zip reads the remaining parts automatically.

Common questions

Do I need to install anything?

The Windows app is a standalone executable with the .NET runtime included. Use 7-Zip to open multipart archives. Windows may show a publisher warning for the test-signed download.

What happens to the queue when I close the app?

The app saves the queue for your SURF account. After sign-in, waiting jobs start automatically and paused jobs stay paused. If an interrupted upload has a local recovery package, use its recovery icon.

Does the app save my password?

Enter your password when you sign in. The Remember username option saves only your username on this PC.
